01 / About Me

Professional Profile

This section introduces who I am, my background, my professional interests, and how this course connects to my career goals.

Who I Am

My name is Sebastian Herrera. I am a Civil Engineer who graduated from the University of South Florida in 2022, and I am currently pursuing a Master Degree in Construction and Facilities Engineering at the University of North Carolina at Charlotte. This portfolio was created for my AI in Construction and Facilities Management course to showcase the assignments, weekly article digests, interactive components, and projects completed throughout the semester.

Professional Interests

My professional interests are focused on practical construction management and field operations, including estimating, scheduling, project controls, safety, concrete work, roadway and bridge construction, documentation, and productivity tracking. I am especially interested in how AI can be used as a real construction tool to improve communication, review documents faster, support planning decisions, organize project information, and help teams work more efficiently without replacing field experience and professional judgment.
